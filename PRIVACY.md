# Codex Remote: data handling

Updated October 17, 2026.

The local MCP server communicates with the native Codex service on your computer. Pairing URLs, manual codes, expiry, device names/IDs, and remote status may be returned to your Codex conversation when requested. Pairing credentials are not saved by this plugin; keep displayed codes and links private. Native Codex and ChatGPT manage authentication, remote relay traffic, and persistent paired-device records under their own policies. The plugin publisher operates no relay, analytics service, or hosted database.

Do not post private chats, credentials, pairing codes, or account identifiers in public issues. For support, use [GitHub issues](https://github.com/Cheaux/codex-remote/issues) with synthetic or redacted examples. This document describes this community project; it does not replace the policies of Codex, ChatGPT, your model provider, or other feature packages.
